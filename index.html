EPUB books, ready to read.

Plainread opens an EPUB book where it is, in a clean, readable window, and does nothing else. No library to import into, no store, no account. Just the book, with its table of contents beside it.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store For the Mac · macOS 14 or later · From the maker of Plainview

A reader, not a library.

Most EPUB apps want to import your books into a library of their own. Plainread opens the book you choose, wherever it is, and shows it the way a good book should look.

Scroll or turn pages

Read the whole book as one smooth scroll, or in pages, two side by side like an open book. Turn them with the arrow keys, a swipe or a click. Switch with ⌘1 and ⌘2 and stay on the same sentence.

Read it your way

Five typefaces: System Serif, System Sans, Georgia, Palatino and Charter. Zoom, set the line width, justify the text, and read in light, sepia or dark. Your choices stick, for every book.

Find your way

The table of contents in a sidebar (⌘T), with the chapter you're reading marked. Find in the whole book, Back after following a footnote, a Recently Read list, and every book opens where you left off.

Copy with Citation

Select a passage and press ⇧⌘C to copy it in quotation marks with the author, the book's title and the printed page, when the book records page numbers. Or choose Chicago, APA, MLA, or just the quotation marks.

Quick Look and Finder icons

Press Space on a book in Finder to see its cover, contents and text without opening anything. Finder shows each book's cover as its icon.

Spotlight, inside your books

Plainread lets Spotlight read the text of the EPUBs on your Mac, so a search finds a phrase in any book, wherever you keep it. Spotlight starts using Plainread after you next log in.

How it works

There's nothing to set up. Open a book and read.

Open a book

Drag an .epub file onto Plainread's window or Dock icon, or use File › Open. The book stays where it is; nothing is copied or imported. Apple Books is the Mac's default app for EPUB files, so to open books with a double-click, make Plainread the default: one click in Plainread, or Get Info › Change All in Finder.

Read

Scroll through the whole book, or turn its pages, two at a time on a wide screen. Jump with the table of contents. Close the window, and Plainread remembers your place.

Or don't even open it

Press Space on a book in Finder for a Quick Look preview, or search inside all your books from Spotlight.

Private by design

There is no Plainread account and no Plainread server. The app collects no data and makes no connections of its own. Books can't run scripts or load anything from the internet in Plainread.

  • No accounts, no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or ads
  • Only reads the books you open
  • Never changes your books
  • Books can't run scripts or reach the web
  • Sandboxed, native Swift app

Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • Opens EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 books: .epub files, and the unzipped EPUB folders Apple Books keeps in iCloud
  • Books without copy protection (DRM). Books bought in Apple Books or other stores with DRM open only in those stores' apps.
